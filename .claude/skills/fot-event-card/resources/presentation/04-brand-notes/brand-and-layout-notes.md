## Dimensions and layout families

All slides are 1920 × 1080 px (16:9). One reference per layout in 02-page-formats:

1. Cover: full-bleed dark bar photo, large lower-left serif title and subtitle.
2. Campaign overview: centered title, description, three event cards interleaved with three photos, handwritten aside.
3. Founders: left text and two product website screenshots; right taped founders' Polaroid.
4. Housekeeping: left bullet list; right taped community group photo over a black-and-white bar photo.
5. Speaker: left taped Polaroid with the speaker portrait laid over it as a separate image; right name and
   biography bullets; italic image caption.
6. Contact/CTA: Instagram and Membership QR pair, workshop QR and handdrawn arrow; right Polaroid/community photo.

## Copy patterns

"Founders on Tap" appears on the cover, overview heading, founder description and farewell caption. The
"founder workshops in bars" proposition repeats on slides 1–2 ("Founder workshops in bars."; "Founder workshops
that go deep on one specific topic in bars."). Short handwritten asides ("a few recent events! :)", "scan to meet
your match!") and bold italic Polaroid captions ("Use agents to SCALE", "Be kind & have fun!!",
"cya at the next founders on tap ❤️") recur.
