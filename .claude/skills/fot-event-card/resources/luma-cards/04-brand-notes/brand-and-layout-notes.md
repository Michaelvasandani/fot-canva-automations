# Observed brand and layout notes — Luma photo cuts

Copied from the Drive doc "Brand and Layout Notes" (Founder's on Tap Advertising › Luma photo cuts › 04 Brand Notes,
https://docs.google.com/document/d/10UsNBjRjxM9sqQZ-oh_PSv7QADV1e10ylLPuSlK7HWA). Source Canva design noted there:

## Dimensions and reusable layout families

All pages are 480 × 480 px, 1:1. PDF media box is 360 × 360 pt (5 × 5 in). These are square event/social cards,
not 1080px Instagram originals. PNGs in 03-page-exports are native-size Canva exports.

1. Pages 1–2: large outlined SF + speaker profile. Two-line wordmark upper left; handwritten topic; looping arrow
   toward a tilted, taped Polaroid at right; large SF lower left; 2X + Y Combinator credential; serif speaker name;
   small biography. Representative: page 1.
2. Pages 3–4: compact outlined SF + speaker profile. Same structure, smaller SF and credentials above the name,
   short biography below. Representative: page 3.
3. Page 5: partner happy-hour card. Wordmark, handwritten event hook, lime arrow, Polaroid photo; partnership line
   and Every logo replace speaker details.
4. Page 6: centered photo-only happy-hour variant. Same background and happy-hour Polaroid; no wordmark, arrow,
   SF or partner block.

Page exports are populated visual references, not blank editable templates. Edit the Canva pages.

## Exact observed colors

- `#202020` charcoal page base (the layered photo makes the visible background vary)
- `#F7F7F7` off-white headings, names and SF outline
- `#FFFFFF` white supporting text
- `#000000` black Polaroid captions
- `#F4FFBC` pale-lime text highlights (e.g. "Columbia & Stanford")
- `#E7F5A2` lime curled-arrow fill
- Beige/off-white tape and paper texture, orange YC mark and white Every mark are in embedded images; no official
  hex codes assigned to them.

## Recurring copy and motifs

- "Founders on Tap" on pages 1–5 (live text, not a logo asset)
- "SF" on pages 1–4
- "happy hour :)" on pages 5–6
- "2X" + Y Combinator credential lockup on pages 1–2
- Structure rather than identical headline: short handwritten topic + speaker name/credentials + italic Polaroid caption
- Taped, tilted, off-white Polaroid on all six pages; frame and tape are baked into the portrait images
- Three off-canvas "LOADING" text elements per page (top ≈ 478 px); not visible copy
