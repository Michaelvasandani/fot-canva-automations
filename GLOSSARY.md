# Glossary

Founders on Tap (FoT) runs founder talks and happy hours, each listed as an event on Luma.

## Event Card

The square image used as an FoT event's cover on Luma. Every Event Card carries the FoT wordmark, the dark bar background and a taped polaroid.

_Avoid_: Luma cover, Luma photo, design, card (unqualified is fine in context)

## Speaker

The person featured on a Speaker Card. Usually a founder, but not always (an advisor or author can speak).

_Avoid_: founder (when meaning the featured person), guest, invitee

## Card Variant

The layout type of an Event Card: **Speaker Card**, **Partner Card** or **Photo-only Card**.

## Speaker Card

An Event Card that features one Speaker: their photo in the polaroid, their name, and the lines that say who they are and what the talk is about.

## Partner Card

An Event Card for an event co-hosted with a partner organisation. It shows a happy-hour photo in the polaroid and the partner's logo under "In partnership with" instead of a Speaker.

## Photo-only Card

An Event Card with only the background and a centred happy-hour polaroid. No wordmark, no Speaker, no partner.

## Master Page

The canonical Canva page for one Card Variant. New Event Cards are always copies of a Master Page; a Master Page itself is never edited to make a card.

## Tagline

The handwritten-style line under the wordmark that names the talk's theme (e.g. "Find your first customer").

## Polaroid Caption

The short, bold phrase on the white strip of the polaroid (e.g. "Start with one").

## Role Line

The small caps line above the Speaker's name saying what they do (e.g. "FOUNDER · PMF ADVISOR", "2X [YC] CEO").

## Credential Logo

An organisation logo shown inline in the Role Line (e.g. Y Combinator).

## Credential Line

The small line under the Speaker's name giving one standout credential (e.g. "Author of The Power of PULL").

## City

The outlined city name on the card (e.g. "SF").
