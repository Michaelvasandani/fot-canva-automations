# FoT Event Card voice

Rules for drafting card copy, taken from the existing cards. The user can edit these.

| Slot | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tagline | 3–6 words naming the talk's theme in plain language. Sentence case, no full stop. Speaks to the founder in the audience ("your", "you"). |
| Polaroid Caption | 2–4 words. A punchy twist on, or answer to, the Tagline. Sentence case, no full stop. |
| Role Line | What the Speaker does, kept short, in capitals. Compact layout: roles joined with ` · `. Logo layout: `<count/qualifier> [logo] <TITLE>`, e.g. "2X [YC] CEO". |
| Speaker name | Full name as they use it publicly. |
| Credential Line | One standout credential, not a CV. The lime highlight goes on the impressive proper noun (school, company, book). |

## Examples

| Speaker | Tagline | Polaroid Caption | Role Line | Credential Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daniel Ahmadizadeh | Find your first customer | Start with one | 2X [YC] CEO | Instructor at **Columbia & Stanford** |
| Jianna Liu | Technical advice from a 2nd time founder | What to build? | 2X [YC] FOUNDER | Talent from MIT, NVIDIA, & Meta |
| Rob Snyder | Why customers actually buy | From push to pull | FOUNDER · PMF ADVISOR | **Author of The Power of PULL** |
| Trevor Keith | Using agents to actually scale | Agents for founders | **FOUNDER OF SOLID** | **Building AI agents that get work done.** |

**Bold** = lime (`#f4ffbc`). The lime highlight is optional: Jianna's Credential Line is all white, and Trevor's Role Line is lime. To make a whole line one colour, `format_text` it with `color` `#f7f7f7` (white) or `#f4ffbc` (lime) after replacing the text.
